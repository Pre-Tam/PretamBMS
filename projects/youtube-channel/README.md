# PreTamBMS YouTube Channel

My YouTube channel is a personal learning and content-creation project focused on explaining **how systems work**.

## About the Channel

The channel explores educational topics across technology, science, and the systems behind things we use or observe.

Current areas of interest include:

- Artificial Intelligence (AI)
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- YouTube and digital platforms
- Technology
- Science
- The universe and how things work

The goal is not simply to create videos, but to learn through research, understand complex topics, and explain them in a clear and accessible way.

## My Role

I personally handle the process of creating the videos, including:

- Researching topics
- Developing the content
- Recording
- Editing
- Publishing

This project also helps me develop practical skills in communication, storytelling, research, content creation, and digital media.
